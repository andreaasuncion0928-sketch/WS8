Our Story

A love story that began in the most unexpected time.

It all started with a simple day, just hanging around, not expecting any love, not even a thought of falling in love. But then, it happened.

Late night conversations and calls, daily chats even from a distance, and a decision to finally meet.

And just like that, we got over with each other — and it all happened in a very sudden way.

Now, it's been more than a decade, and our story is still our favorite one. ♡
